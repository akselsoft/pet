01 / The novel

Two lives. Shared experiences.

Elora and Aidan grow up together. Elora is human. Aidan is a constructed intelligence designed to perceive and evaluate the world independently, without a human body.

As their lives unfold, the boundaries between observation, participation and experience become increasingly significant. Pattern Witness explores not what it means for a machine to become human, but what it means for one existence to have an impact on another.
